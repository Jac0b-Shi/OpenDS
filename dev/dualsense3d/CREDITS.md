The prepared asset separates the shell and controls and preserves the original
geometry, UVs and textures. This OpenDS adaptation adds its own native OpenGL
renderer, physically based lighting, shadow map, clear-cap material adjustment,
smooth control animation, hit testing, touch-surface sampling and live input
integration. It is an independent visual recreation, not dimensional CAD.

Grey Camouflage placement, control colors and finish are informed by
[Sony's official product views](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/accessories/dualsense-wireless-controller/).
Its front-panel print and geometric micro-pattern are generated by this
renderer; their layout approximates the edition's appearance. Product photos
are not bundled as textures. The rear remains plain grey.

The upstream site's scripts are not part of this renderer. The upstream
attribution also mentions that site's Three.js and diagnostics dependencies;
those packages are not included in this application.

PyOpenGL 3.1.10 is bundled privately under its BSD-style license; see
[vendor/PyOpenGL-LICENSE.txt](vendor/PyOpenGL-LICENSE.txt). NumPy, GTK and Cairo
are supplied by Ubuntu. OpenDS application code retains the repository's
GPL-3.0-or-later license; the frozen backend includes its license and notices.
